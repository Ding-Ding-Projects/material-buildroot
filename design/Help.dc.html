Create your first image

About 10 minutes to read · works offline

An image is a complete, bootable Linux system built from your choices. The guide walks you through five stages, and you can switch between Easy and Advanced at any time without losing your draft.

1. Choose a purpose

Pick what the device should do. The purpose sets sensible defaults for packages and size.

lightbulbYou can open this step directly from the guide with the info button beside each control.

2. Pick a target

Choose a board or virtual machine. Board presets fill in the architecture and boot method.
