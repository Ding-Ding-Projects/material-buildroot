# Fresh-eye review: Material Browser and Material Buildroot screens

Frames looked at (all with Read): browser/{Main,Launch,Palette,Safety,Settings,SuperProfiles,Sync,Vault} x {light, -dark, -phone}; buildroot/{Main,Builds,Tools,Help,Overlays,Palette,Safety,Settings} x {light, -dark, -phone}; gold/{gmail,finder,static-card,dark-hero}. Paths below are relative to ``. Zoom crops I made to confirm two items are in `revzoom/`.

Judged as real product screens, not marketing scenes. Chrome height, real headings, string counts, stage bleed and motion were not counted as findings.

## Beside the closest gold: the one difference

- **Material Browser, Main vs gmail:** Gmail gives one list most of the window, in one surface with hairline dividers. Browser splits the width five ways (rail, Tabs pane, list, detail pane, plus window gutters) and draws every row as its own tinted tile. The list gets about 45% of the width against Gmail's 70%, and two of the five columns are half empty.
- **Material Buildroot, Builds/Main vs finder:** Finder is one full-height list that runs off the bottom. Buildroot's Main has no list at all: three cards and a stepper, then a 150px empty band. Builds, the closest screen to Finder, stops after three job rows and leaves a 90px gap under them. Its log shows four lines in a box built for ten.
- **Restraint vs static-card:** static-card has one hero and one satellite that carry the point. Browser Vault centers two equal cards with even margins on a blank window, so neither one is the hero.

## Framing (ranked)

1. **Browser, Sync, phone (`browser/Sync-phone.png`).** The page layout breaks. The conflict pane opens beside the main pane instead of over it, so the main pane is squeezed to a 60px strip and its text breaks letter by letter ("Sy", "Encr", "[Y AD"). Screenshot the whole left strip. Fix: below 600px, show the conflict pane as a full-screen sheet or a pushed route and hide the main pane.
2. **Both apps, Settings (`browser/Settings.png`, `buildroot/Settings.png`, and the dark versions).** The two screens are the same: the same sections, the same rows (funny level, emojis in dialogs, Focus modes, Application name), only recolored. Neither screen could tell you which product it is. Buildroot has no build settings (download cache, toolchain, output directory, parallel jobs), and Browser has no browser settings (default engine, extension policy). Screenshot the two side by side. Fix: put each product's own settings group first, and move the shared language and focus rows lower.
3. **Both apps, Settings: empty band under "Voice and sound" (`browser/Settings.png`, `buildroot/Settings.png`).** The right column ends at y≈500, and "Focus modes" starts at y≈790 because it shares a grid row with the much taller Language card. That leaves 290px of empty space mid-window. Fix: use two independent masonry columns, or one column of sections.
4. **Browser, Main (`browser/Main.png`, `browser/Main-dark.png`).** Two panes stop well above their bottoms. The Tabs pane is empty from y≈360 to y≈860 (about 500px), and the detail pane is empty from y≈785 to y≈910. Fix: remove the Tabs pane on this screen, or fold it into the rail, and give the width to the list. Fill the detail pane with activity or revisions, or shorten it.
5. **Browser, SuperProfiles (`browser/SuperProfiles.png`).** The template list holds three items, then is empty from y≈470 to y≈850. Revision history is empty from y≈685 to y≈910. Fix: narrow the list pane, or show more templates and recent sessions. Let the revision list run to the bottom.
6. **Buildroot, Main (`buildroot/Main.png`).** The content ends at y≈760 in a pane that ends at y≈910. The Tabs pane is empty from y≈400 to y≈860. Fix: add the queue, recent builds or a package list under the stepper, or bring the log in.
7. **Buildroot, Tools and Help (`buildroot/Tools.png`, `buildroot/Help.png`).** The converter queue ends at y≈692, leaving 220px empty. The Help article ends at y≈672 after section 2, but its "On this page" lists four sections and its intro promises five stages. Fix: show the rest of the article (Configure, Build and verify) so it runs off the pane. Fill the converter pane with history.
8. **Browser, Vault (`browser/Vault.png`, `browser/Vault-dark.png`).** Two equal cards float in the middle of an empty window, with about 150px blank above and 160px below. The screen also asks for two tasks at once: Unlock and the first-run wizard (whose step 1 says the vault is "Done" while the other card asks for its passphrase). Fix: show one task per screen. Run the wizard first, or show unlock alone, with the other as a secondary link.
9. **Component sheets: dialogs stretched to equal height (`browser/Safety.png`, `buildroot/Safety.png`).** Each dialog is padded to the tallest one. "Delete Research?" is empty from y≈536 to y≈672, "Play your way out" from y≈508 to y≈672, and Support Tickets from y≈500 to y≈672. As real dialogs they would hug their content. Fix: use height auto, top aligned.

## Hierarchy and density

10. **Placeholder tokens on real surfaces (many frames).** I saw `[DATE]` (Browser Main detail "Created", SuperProfiles revision 7, Sync conflict rows), `[UPDATED-AT]` (title bar or footer on every Browser and Buildroot screen), `[DEVICE NAME]` and `[YOUR SERVER ADDRESS]` (`browser/Sync.png`), `[PHOTO]` (`browser/Palette.png` and `buildroot/Overlays.png` toasts), `[DISH PHOTO]` (`buildroot/Safety.png`, the focal image of the quiz), and `[COMMIT]` and `[DIM SUM NAME]` (`buildroot/Overlays.png` About). Fix: use plausible values ("12 Oct 2026", "nas.local:8443", a real dish photo) or the real formatted string.
11. **Buildroot: error tone is the heaviest thing on screen, worst in dark (`buildroot/Main-dark.png`, `buildroot/Builds-dark.png`, `buildroot/Tools-dark.png`).** The router lab card, the interrupted job row and the stopped rootfs row are each a full saturated red fill (about #8B0000) in dark. They outweigh the running build and the primary action. Fix: use errorContainer at its real M3 dark tone (a muted deep red with onErrorContainer text), or a tonal card with only the icon and action in error.
12. **Browser: running status has no consistent color (`browser/Main.png`, `browser/SuperProfiles.png`).** Work "Running" has a near-black dot and Testing "Running" has a maroon dot (white and pink in dark). "Open" on Dev workstation is black. Stopped is a gray icon. Two rows in the same state look different. Fix: one status token per state, used everywhere (for example, primary for running and outline for stopped).
13. **Browser, SuperProfiles: Added/Changed/Removed are all the same small gray label (`browser/SuperProfiles.png`).** Only the icons differ, and in the "In this template" list everything says "Changed". Fix: color or weight the change kind (tertiary for added, error for removed), or drop the redundant label.
14. **Browser, Launch: the primary button looks enabled while gated (`browser/Launch.png`).** "Launch Firefox" is a full primary button next to "One review left to confirm". Fix: show the disabled filled style until both boxes are checked, or make the gate text the button's supporting line.
15. **Browser, Launch: a review checklist in error pink (`browser/Launch.png`, `browser/Launch-dark.png`).** "Review before launch" is an acknowledgement, not an error, but it gets the errorContainer fill, maroon in dark. Fix: use tertiaryContainer or a surface card. Keep error tone for errors.
16. **Browser, Main: the split "Launch" button sits on Research, not the selected Work row (`browser/Main.png`).** It reads as a hover state with no pointer, and the right-hand action column is inconsistent: one row has Launch, the others have a kebab. Fix: show Launch on every stopped row, or only on the selected row.
17. **Browser, Main dark: the info note outweighs the primary action (`browser/Main-dark.png`).** The "Browser data stays encrypted" note is a saturated filled purple block, while "Show window" is pale lilac. Fix: put the note on surfaceContainerHigh with a primary icon.
18. **Both apps: icon blobs with no meaning (`browser/SuperProfiles.png` "Session open", `buildroot/Builds.png` running job).** A scalloped purple or blue cookie shape with no glyph inside sits where a status icon should be. Fix: put a real glyph in the shape (play, terminal), or use the M3 Expressive loading indicator if it means "in progress".
19. **Buildroot, Main: three cards with three different treatments (`buildroot/Main.png`).** qemu appliance and kiosk rpi4 have an outline and a shadow, and router lab is a flat fill. Outline plus shadow is a redundant tonal border. Fix: pick one card style (filled or elevated, not outlined plus elevated), and let state change only the fill. The qemu card also ends its content at y≈376 in a card that runs to y≈436.
20. **Buildroot, Palette: a contradictory state (`buildroot/Palette.png`).** The light-theme frame shows "Theme · Dark" switched on. Fix: show the switch off in light, or use a theme the frame is not in.
21. **Buildroot, Overlays: a doubled selection mark (`buildroot/Overlays.png` setup dialog).** The WSL 2 option has both a filled radio and a check badge. Fix: drop the check badge.
22. **Browser, Safety, "Play your way out": two segments look selected (`browser/Safety.png`).** "Dim sum" has a check on a tonal fill, and "Sums" is the filled pill. Fix: one selected segment in the connected button group.
23. **Selection color changes between surfaces (`browser/Palette.png`, `buildroot/Overlays.png`).** The context-menu highlight is pink (tertiary) while the palette, list and nav selection is lavender or blue (secondaryContainer). In the blue Buildroot app, the pink "Pin tab" highlight reads as a foreign accent. Fix: use the same secondaryContainer for menu focus as for every other selection.

## Polish

24. **Browser, Main: the floating selection toolbar touches the last row (`browser/Main.png`, zoom `revzoom/toolbar.png`).** The toolbar's top edge sits about 2px under the descenders of "Firefox · No automation" and covers the bottom of the Travel tile. Fix: add bottom padding to the list equal to the toolbar height plus 16px.
25. **Browser: the "S" avatar reads as clipped (`browser/Main.png`, `browser/Main-phone.png`, zoom `revzoom/avatars.png`).** The M3 Expressive "arch" shape has a flat bottom that lands on the row's baseline, so it looks cut off. Mixing arch, cookie, sunny and circle shapes per profile reads as random, not as identity. Fix: drop the arch from the avatar shape set, or use a single shape family for avatars.
26. **Browser, Settings phone: "Change" collides with the path (`browser/Settings-phone.png`, Database row).** "Change" overlaps "Documents\Passwords.kdbx". Fix: move the trailing action below the text on narrow widths, or truncate the path with an ellipsis.
27. **Phone list items lose their trailing controls (`browser/Main-phone.png`, `browser/SuperProfiles-phone.png`, `browser/Settings-phone.png`, `buildroot/Builds-phone.png`, `buildroot/Tools-phone.png`, `buildroot/Overlays-phone.png` Accessibility tools).** Switches, kebabs, status chips and folder icons wrap onto their own line under the label, left aligned. This is not M3 list anatomy. Fix: keep one trailing slot that stays on the right, and truncate or wrap only the supporting text.
28. **Phone: connected button groups and toolbars wrap (`browser/Settings-phone.png` Bilingual, `browser/Vault-phone.png` Next under Back, `buildroot/Main-phone.png` filter chips over three rows, `buildroot/Help-phone.png` breadcrumb over three lines).** Fix: scroll chip rows horizontally. Keep Back and Next on one row with Skip as a text button. Shorten the breadcrumb to a back arrow and title.
29. **Phone: palettes and builder labels break (`browser/Palette-phone.png`, `buildroot/Palette-phone.png`).** "Regex builder · this search only" wraps to three to five lines, the last palette row is cut under the footer, and in `browser/Palette-phone.png` the snackbar and dim sum toast sit on top of the Notifications card text. Fix: put the builder label on its own line, make the palette a full-height sheet on phones, and stack toasts above the bottom nav, never over content.
30. **Buildroot, Builds phone: the log is clipped on two sides (`buildroot/Builds-phone.png`).** The text runs off the right ("Installing to") and the fourth line is cut by the box bottom. The "Last build succeeded · image hash recorded" line in `buildroot/Main-phone.png` also runs to the card edge. Fix: let the log scroll horizontally inside an inset, and wrap or truncate the card line inside the 16px inset.
31. **Browser, Sync: the last row runs into the pane edge (`browser/Sync.png`).** Shopping conflict resolved ends at y≈900 in a pane that ends at y≈912, which is less bottom inset than the pane's 32px sides. Fix: keep the pane's inset at the bottom, or let the list scroll under a fade.
32. **Browser, Sync: the storage progress has no visible remaining track in light (`browser/Sync.png`).** The wavy indicator stops at about 60%, and the rest is only a lone stop dot. The dark frame shows the track. Fix: use the light-theme secondaryContainer track color.
33. **Delete slider looks disabled (`browser/Safety.png`).** The "Then slide all the way" slider is a gray track with a 3px handle sitting apart at the left. It reads as a disabled progress bar, not an M3 slider. In `buildroot/Safety-dark.png` the inactive track is darker red than the active one, so it looks reversed. Fix: use the M3 Expressive slider tokens (primary or error active track, container inactive track) in both themes.
34. **Destructive dialogs end with no confirm action (`browser/Safety.png` Delete, `buildroot/Safety.png` Clean build output).** The only button is "Emergency exit". If the slider is the commit, say so ("Slide to delete"). Otherwise add the destructive filled button.
35. **The rail's top action changes meaning per screen (`browser/Main.png` add-person, `browser/Sync.png` sync, `buildroot/Builds.png` play; missing on `browser/Settings.png`, `buildroot/Tools.png` and `buildroot/Help.png`).** On Sync it repeats the "Sync now" button. The rail items also shift vertically when it disappears (Profiles at y≈217 vs y≈213). Fix: keep one FAB slot that is always present, and give it the primary create action for each destination, or hide it with its space reserved.
36. **Title-bar version line moves around (`browser/Settings.png` and `browser/Vault.png` show "0.1.1 · updated [UPDATED-AT]" under the app name; Main shows it in the footer).** Fix: put it in one place, the footer or About.
37. **Accessibility tools: "Text size" wraps onto two lines (`buildroot/Overlays.png`).** It is squeezed by A−/100%/A+. Fix: put the stepper on its own row, or reduce it to an icon stepper.
38. **Two neutral families (`browser/Sync.png` "This computer" card, `browser/SuperProfiles.png` Session card).** These cards use a cool gray (surfaceVariant) inside lavender panes. Fix: use the surfaceContainer roles from the same tonal palette.

## Not M3 / M3 Expressive anatomy (summary)

- Menus built as separate shadowed boxes with 2px gaps (`browser/Palette.png`, `buildroot/Overlays.png`). M3 Expressive grouped menus share one container with gaps, not individually shadowed slabs.
- List items whose trailing controls drop below the label on phone (item 27).
- The gray "disabled-looking" slider (item 33).
- Every list row drawn as its own tinted tile (Main, Settings, Sync, Builds). This is closer to M3 Expressive's segmented list than to a generic look, but with no dividers it reads as a stack of cards. Fine if intended; the gold comparison above is the cost.

## What I could not check

- I had only the PNGs, so no live states: hover, focus rings, keyboard focus order, reduced motion, and the M3 Expressive shape morphs and wavy-progress animation were not seen.
- Whether the Browser and Buildroot icons match the real app icon assets. I had no source icon to compare.
- Exact pixel insets and contrast ratios. I measured by eye from the frames, with two zoom crops.
- High-contrast, compact density and the bilingual and Cantonese modes. There were no frames for them.
- Native Windows behavior (snap layouts, title-bar drag region, caption button hover).
